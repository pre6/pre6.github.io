# Tools as a means of Data Compression

I had an epiphany at work one day. I had successfully identified an area of my work where a tool could be made, and then suddenly
I understood data compression. To understand my thought process lets go back all the way to the beginning. I can do anything I want on this page,
so I can do that, tell a story from the beginning.

When I was a kid, I had always tried to understand how our brains perceived reality. As a result, I went into Neuroscience in my undergrad (It was that or
art school, but that's a whole other story). Upon studying Neuroscience, I quickly (or not so quickly) realized that we had no biological basis for
consciousness. I briefly switched to philosophy. Then one night I went to a neuroscience conference where I listened to a talk given by Geoffrey Hinton.
He went through the basis of backpropagation and also berated us for not knowing calculus. I had taken AP Calculus so I understood the basis of what he was 
talking about. But still had the same realization that we don't know much at all. There was no concrete evidence (at the time) that cells communicated backwards.
But the idea was more compelling because it felt actionable. I could build it on my own and test it on my own. Building something meant that you understood someting.
It was the challenge that I wanted. Therefore, I took one computer science class, and many mathematics and statistics courses on the side. I still kept my 
major as Neuroscience and Physiology because, well, I don't know.
