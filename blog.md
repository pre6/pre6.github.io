# My Thoughts

Hi my name is Preethi and I have a lot of thoughts about various different computer science related topics. I am mostly interested in how topics of computer science and complexity science informs how we construct realities in out heads. Since a young age I have always been interested in understanding how our brains form consciousness. As a result, I went into Neuroscience in my undergrad. Swept away by the math behind machine learning, I got my masters in data science. And now I am working as a Data Integration Specialist, making data infrastructure, while trying to get my thoughts in a row to really find a strong pathway of research in the future. 

As an attempt to motivate myself, I have chosen this place to consolidate all my thoughts, my big thoughts into one place. After countless journals and loose papers and github repos, I will sort out my ideas here. 

This is an AI free spot on the internet. I do not use AI in my writing because it defeats the purpose, the purpose being thinking.
